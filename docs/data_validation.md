## Approximate checks

| Skater shots = team shots | 11,726 | 97.6% | 99.4% (within 2) | CHN's team shot total sometimes counts shots it didn't credit to a player |

| Skater PIM + bench and goalie PIM = team PIM | 11,726 | 99.7% | 99.9% (within 2) | Player PIM and team PIM count misconducts and coincidental minors differently |
| Skater faceoffs won = team faceoffs won | 11,726 | 98.6% | 99.2% (within 2) | Some faceoffs aren't credited to a player |
